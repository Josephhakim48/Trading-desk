How this clock works

These are the regular forex session conventions from your original desk. Each runs Monday–Friday in its city’s calendar.

Session schedules in each city’s local time
SessionLocal hoursTime zone
Asia / Tokyo09:00–18:00Asia/Tokyo

London and New York adjust for daylight saving independently. The overlap uses the intersection of their actual timestamps. +1d means the closing time is on the next display-zone date.

The map shows your selected local date. The white marker is now; colored bands are scheduled sessions. Asia means Tokyo here. Sydney is not tracked, so “between sessions” does not mean the entire forex market is closed.

This clock shows a schedule, not prices or a broker’s trading availability. Public holidays and broker maintenance are not included. Example broker hours · UK clock changes · US clock changes
